# Privacy policy

Updated October 29, 2026.

Jarvis Backups is a personal utility operated by its account owner for private disaster recovery.

## Google Drive access

The utility uses Google's `drive.file` permission to create, read, verify, and remove its own backup files and folders. It may also access files explicitly opened with the application. It does not request general access to unrelated Drive files, Gmail, Google Photos, or contacts.

## Data handling

Backup archives are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload to Google Drive. The utility uses file names, sizes, dates, and checksums to manage and verify recovery copies. Authorization credentials and encryption keys are stored privately on the owner's devices. Recovery keys are kept separately from the cloud archives.

Google stores the encrypted archives under the owner's Google account. Data is used only for the owner's backup, verification, retention, and recovery. The utility does not sell data, use it for advertising, share it with other users, or use it to train AI models. This public information site does not contain backup files or recovery credentials. GitHub, which hosts these pages, may process ordinary website request information under its own privacy policies.

## Retention and control

The planned cloud retention is up to seven daily Home Assistant backups and the latest verified Docker recovery archive. Older copies are removed only after replacement copies are verified. Local retention may differ. The owner can revoke the application's Google access, delete its backup folder, or stop the utility at any time.

## Contact

This is a single-owner utility, not a public service. The Google account owner operates the application and manages all data requests and settings.
